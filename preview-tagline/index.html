The brand system

Three words. One promise.

Evidence, model and trust become the keywords in all marketing and product marketing. The model sits in the middle, crossed out, because it is never the point. Hover or tap each word.

EvidenceWhat we deliver
Every control, every agent action and every decision leaves proof that is mapped to the standards and regulations a firm answers to. This is Evidence by Design, your method from last year.
→
ModelNever the point
Models change every month. Frontier, open-weight and local models come and go. No single model is trusted, so the value cannot live in the model.
→
TrustWhat the client gets
Proof for shareholders, clients, employees, regulators and families. Trust is the holy grail. Security, privacy and compliance are how it is earned.

How we present it

Five creative ideas

1 · The swap

Models change, evidence stays. The demo at the top of this page: the model name keeps changing while the evidence column never moves. Works as the website hero and as a 15-second video.

2 · The receipt

Every agent action prints a receipt. A clean receipt for each AI decision: what was asked, which rule applied, who approved, where the proof is kept. A strong visual for decks and the AI Governance Review.

3 · The boardroom question

"Can you show me?" Open every pitch with the question a board, auditor or regulator asks. The answer on the next slide is the evidence pack, not a model diagram.

4 · The scale

Evidence outweighs the model. A simple balance: the model on one side, the evidence on the other, and the evidence side always heavier. Works as an icon and a LinkedIn banner.

5 · Banks are saying it too

The UOB proof point. UOB's research says AI-driven payments depend less on advances in AI than on how well institutions govern it. We quote the source and let the tagline answer it.

Protecting it

What I found, and the next step

A short phrase like a tagline is protected as a trademark, not a copyright. Copyright does not cover short phrases. A trademark protects a name or tagline used to sell services.

What my web search found on 31 October 2026. This is a first check, not a legal search:

  • "Evidence, not the model" and "Evidence, not models": I found no company or brand using either line. The only close hits were academic articles on models versus evidence, such as Boston Review and Law of Markets, and an arXiv paper.
  • "Evidence by Design": I found no brand using it. But the very close phrase "evidence-based design" is common in architecture, healthcare and product design. That may make "Evidence by Design" harder to register than the tagline.
  • I have not searched the trademark office databases yet. That is the real check.

Next steps: 1. Search the official databases: the US trademark office, Thailand's Department of Intellectual Property and WIPO's Global Brand Database. 2. Choose the service classes, such as software and consulting. 3. File. Filing costs money, so it waits for your approval. I recommend a trademark attorney for the filing.
