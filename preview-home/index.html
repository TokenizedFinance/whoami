Custodians, exchanges, banks, payment firms and asset managers hold other people's money. Their boards, auditors, banking partners and regulators all ask the same question: can you prove your controls work, every day? The firm that proves its trust first wins the mandates, the licences and the partners.

What we do

Security first, privacy first, confidentiality first and compliance first, in every design.

AI Governance Review. A three-week review of the AI systems a regulated firm runs, mapped to NIST, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 27001 and the regulator's guidance, then monthly evidence that each system follows your rules.

Agentic AI architecture. Agent roles, orchestration, data, identity and policy as code, through to deployment, secure from day one.

Tokenized finance and digital assets. Custody, wallets, tokenization and settlement, with compliance enforced at the token level.

Fractional security leadership. Security architecture and virtual CISO support for fintechs and financial firms.
